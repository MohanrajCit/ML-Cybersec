Probability-Based Risk Mapping (No Model Retraining)

Original Binary Model → Three-Level Risk Output

                    P(HIGH) from Binary RF Classifier
                    ↓
        ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  0%                                  100%  │
        └───────────────────────────────────────────┘
         ↓
         │
         ├─── 0.00 to 0.34 ───→ LOW RISK
         │                      Confidence = 1.0 - P(HIGH)
         │                      ✅ Strong evidence of low severity
         │
         ├─── 0.35 to 0.69 ───→ MEDIUM RISK
         │                      Confidence = P(HIGH)
         │                      ⚠️  Model uncertainty / moderate threat
         │
         └─── 0.70 to 1.00 ───→ HIGH RISK
                                Confidence = P(HIGH)
                                🚨 Strong evidence of critical risk


EXAMPLE CLASSIFICATIONS
========================

CVE Description                              P(HIGH)  →  Risk      Confidence
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Critical RCE with root privileges"         0.92     →  HIGH      92%
"Zero-day exploit in authentication"        0.78     →  HIGH      78%

WHY THESE THRESHOLDS?
====================

70% Threshold (HIGH)
--------------------
✓ Industry standard for "high confidence" ML predictions
✓ Minimizes false positives for critical alerts
✓ Above this: model is quite certain about HIGH risk

35% Threshold (MEDIUM/LOW boundary)
------------------------------------
✓ Below 35%: model is reasonably confident it's NOT high risk
✓ Between 35-69%: model shows significant uncertainty
✓ Captures the "gray zone" requiring human judgment

MEDIUM Zone Purpose
-------------------
✓ Acknowledges ML uncertainty rather than forcing binary decisions
✓ Flags CVEs needing additional investigation
✓ Provides actionable triage signal for security teams
✓ More realistic than overconfident HIGH/LOW classifications


CONFIDENCE CALCULATION
=======================

For HIGH and MEDIUM:
  confidence = P(HIGH)
  → Directly reflects the model's belief in high risk

For LOW:
  confidence = 1.0 - P(HIGH) = P(LOW)
  → Reflects the model's belief in low risk
